      About VAYU

      VAYU V3

      VAYU is a production-ready air quality, weather, preventive health, AYUSH wellness, and AI safety assistant built to help people make safer daily decisions.

      What VAYU V3 provides

      VAYU combines live AQI, local weather, pollutant breakdowns, health-risk scoring, daily safety planning, remedy monitoring, device alerts, and an AI assistant into one clean PWA experience.

      Live IntelligenceLocation-based AQI, temperature, humidity, wind, rain, and forecast insights.
      Health SafetyPersonal risk score, mask advice, outdoor activity guidance, and exposure tracking.
      AYUSH WellnessPreventive wellness routines, safe remedies, and daily progress monitoring.
      Smart AssistantContext-aware chatbot for AQI, weather, remedies, travel, and safety questions.
      Production BasePWA support, backend login, JSON database, deployment configs, privacy and security docs.

      Safety note: VAYU gives preventive wellness guidance, not medical diagnosis or emergency treatment. Users should consult a doctor for serious or persistent symptoms.
